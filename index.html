Work

Will it get in?
Tool for anyone buying or moving big furniture, 2026
Enter a sofa's, wardrobe's or mattress's size and the tight spot on the way in (a hallway corner or a door), and see whether it gets through, how to carry it (flat, tilted or on its end), and what would fit.
One oven, one dinner
Tool for anyone cooking a big dinner, 2026
List your dishes with the times and temperatures from your recipes and get a cooking timeline that fits your oven, your hob and your hands. When it can't all fit, it says exactly what gives and why.
Will your best player win?
Tool for tournament organizers, 2026
Running a tournament for your club, office or friends? Enter your players, tables and time, and see how often a knockout, double elimination, Swiss, groups or a round robin crowns your best player. Then print the schedule.
How do you know 7 is the best?
Interactive essay, 2026
How a computer proves that a puzzle can't be solved in fewer moves: search trees, budgets, and "ghost light", on a mirror puzzle you can play.
Mirrors that do logic
Interactive essay, 2026
Why no program is expected to solve every mirror puzzle quickly: build logic gates out of mirrors, then turn any logic formula into a board you can play.
No Fewer
Puzzle game, 2026
You don't solve the puzzle: you prove nobody can solve it in fewer flips, one forced mirror at a time. 28 levels and a daily puzzle, in the browser.
Counterexample
Puzzle game, 2026
Each level makes a claim about a famous algorithm that sounds right. You build the input that breaks it, and the second star asks for the smallest such input, proven by trying every smaller one. 15 levels, in the browser.
Squeeze
Puzzle game, 2026
Each level asks for the most, or the fewest: dance pairs, water through pipes, stacks of boxes. You build an answer, then the proof that nobody can do better, and the level is solved when the two meet. 19 levels, in the browser.
The Imp’s Scale
Puzzle game, 2026
Find the heaviest, the runner-up, the middle one or the fake coin in the fewest weighings possible, against an Imp that has no hidden answer and decides each one to hurt you most. Every budget is the proven minimum. 20 levels, in the browser.
Which sport finds the better player?
Interactive essay, 2026
A scoring system is a test of who is better. Tennis, table tennis, badminton and volleyball against the proven best test, which no major sport plays for a whole match. Design your own format and see how close you get.
Guess the next card
Interactive essay, 2026
How many riffle shuffles make a deck random? Play "guess the next card" against a deck shuffled three times, next to a guesser that knows the exact odds, and see what the deck still remembers.
Solve it, then prove it
Experiment notes, 2026
Nine language models get the same mirror puzzles twice: once to solve, once to prove nobody can do it in fewer flips. A program checks every answer. Includes what went wrong in the measuring.
untilsure
Command-line tool, 2026
You fixed a flaky test. Did it work? untilsure reruns it until a sequential test can say FIXED or NOT FIXED, with the chance of a wrong verdict computed exactly, and keeps the logs of the failures.
